1. Handgun
Simple one handed weapon used for combat. Has no special mechanics.
- Damage: Weak
- Fire Rate: Medium
- Magazine Size: 12
- Reload Time: Fast

2. Boomstick
Sawed off shotgun, has strong blast that can knock back enemies and propel the player in the opposite direction.
- Damage: Medium, AoE, Knockback
- Fire Rate: Medium
- Magazine Size: 1 (upgradeable to 2)
- Reload Time: Medium

3. Harpoon Launcher
Buccaneering weapon that fires a harpoon tied by a string, for pulling enemies in or grappling to a higher position.
- Damage: Strong
- Fire Rate: -
- Magazine Size: 1
- Reload Time: Slow

4. RPG Launcher
Large explosive launcher with long cooldown, for knocking down breakable walls.
- Damage: Extreme, AoE, Can hurt self, Knockback
- Fire Rate: -
- Magazine Size: 1
- Reload Time: Snail

4. Assault MG
Fast firing rifle, capable of keeping the player suspended in air if aimed downward.
- Damage: Weak
- Fire Rate: Fast
- Magazine Size: 30
- Reload Time: Fast

6. Flamethrower
Spews out ignited gas, burns down anything in it's path.
- Damage: Weak, DoT
- Fire Rate: Rapid
- Magazine Size: 50
- Reload Time: Slow

7. Sniper Rifle
Extreme long distance damage, can hit buttons and switches outside the range of the player.
- Damage: Extreme
- Fire Rate: Slow
- Magazine Size: 5
- Reload Time: Medium
