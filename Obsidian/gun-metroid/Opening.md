Your ship is under attack. You and your crew had just reached orbit of an alien planet, and within seconds the planet had opened fire, damaging the thrusters. Running through the halls, explosions quickly catching up behind you, you arrive at the barracks, only to find a single handgun remaining. Pick it up. Within seconds, another explosion occurs in the barracks, sealing you in. Aliens emerge from the walls, surrounding you on all sides. Open fire. Frantically trying to defend yourself. Another explosion opens the door, you sprint out in a desperate attempt to reach the bridge. Another explosion in front of you, you black out.

You're falling.

You look up at your ship as you plummet to the surface. In a brilliant flash of light, the remains of your ship explode, debris flying at mach speed past you. You fall unconscious once more as you crash onto the unknown planet.
